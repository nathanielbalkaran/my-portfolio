# Investment Thesis

Apple Inc. (AAPL) represents a core holding for long-term growth and capital preservation. This note summarizes our view on the business, valuation, and key risks.

## Business Overview

Apple’s ecosystem—hardware, services, and software—creates durable switching costs and pricing power. Services growth continues to diversify revenue and improve margins.

## Valuation

We use a blended approach: DCF, peer multiples, and scenario analysis. Our base case implies a mid-teens total return over a 3–5 year horizon from current levels.

## Risks

Key risks include iPhone cyclicality, regulatory pressure on App Store and services, and competition in China.
